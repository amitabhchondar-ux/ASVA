# Code of Conduct

ASVA is a small student project, and we want it to be a friendly place to learn, build and ask questions, whether you are a first-year student or a senior engineer. This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) (version 2.1), written in our own words.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to take part without harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We will act in ways that keep the community open, welcoming and healthy.

## What we encourage

- Being patient and kind, especially with beginners
- Respecting different opinions, skills and experiences
- Giving feedback that is specific, constructive and about the work, not the person
- Admitting mistakes, saying sorry, and learning from them
- Crediting other people's ideas and work
- Thinking about what is best for the whole community, not only for yourself

## What is not acceptable

- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Insults, put-downs, trolling, or personal and political attacks
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including repeated unwanted contact
- Publishing someone's private information (address, phone number, email, photos, **voice recordings**) without their consent
- Submitting audio recordings of people who have not agreed to it, or any content that breaks the law or someone's privacy
- Pressuring people to accept a change, or repeatedly reopening a closed decision
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

## Responsibilities of maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for explaining and applying these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and will explain why when it is appropriate to do so. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently ban someone for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

## Where this applies

This code covers all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chats, and any official group channel) and also applies when someone is representing the project in public, for example presenting it at a college event or hackathon.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer at **amitabhchondar@gmail.com**. Please include links or screenshots where possible. Reports are handled promptly and treated confidentially. A maintainer who is involved in the issue will not be part of deciding the outcome.

## How we respond

1. **Correction:** a private note or a public clarification, with an explanation of what was inappropriate and a request to change.
2. **Warning:** a formal warning with consequences if the behaviour continues.
3. **Temporary ban:** a set period with no interaction in project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations or serious harassment.

## Related policies

- Security issues should be reported through [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not here.
- Academic integrity rules of your institution still apply; if you contribute as part of coursework, follow your instructor's guidance on collaboration.
